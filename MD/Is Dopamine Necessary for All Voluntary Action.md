# How Strongly Is the Claim Supported That Dopamine Is Necessary for the Initiation of Voluntary Action?

> **Correction note.** An earlier draft of this reference repeated the popular gloss that dopamine depletion "preserves hedonic liking" — i.e., that pleasure is left intact while only motivation fails. That framing overstates the evidence and silently upgrades Berridge's guarded, scare-quoted technical term "liking" (an objective reflex measure he himself distinguishes from conscious pleasure) into a claim about subjective hedonic experience. The human experimental evidence runs the *other* way. This version states the action-initiation findings (solid) and the hedonic findings (which do **not** support "liking intact" in humans) at their correct, separate strengths.

## TL;DR
- **Solid:** In rodents, near-total absence of dopamine abolishes self-initiated goal-directed action — animals starve next to available food — and restoring dopamine specifically to the dorsal striatum (caudate-putamen) rescues it. Dopamine is necessary for the routine, self-initiated, effortful **execution** of motivated voluntary action under normal conditions. Established neuroscience.
- **The "liking is preserved" claim does NOT transfer to humans and should not be asserted.** The rodent evidence for preserved "liking" is orofacial taste reactivity — a brainstem reflex preserved even in *decerebrate* rats — measuring a reflex arc below anything meant by hedonic experience, not felt pleasure. The strongest *human experimental* evidence (D2-blockade RCTs; Parkinson's reward-sensitivity studies) shows dopaminergic disruption **reduces** reported and physiological hedonic response. In humans, anhedonia is a real loss of pleasure, not a motivational illusion atop intact enjoyment.
- **Overreach to avoid in both directions:** (a) "Dopamine *is* pleasure" — too strong; liking has a real opioid-mediated component dissociable from dopaminergic wanting under specific manipulations. (b) "Dopamine is irrelevant to pleasure / liking is intact" — also wrong for humans, and the version weaponized to dismiss anhedonia as theatrical. Defensible position: **wanting and liking are partially separable systems that normally operate together; dopamine is the substrate of wanting, effort, and action-initiation, and in humans its disruption also demonstrably degrades felt pleasure.**

## Key Findings

1. **Dopamine-deficient (DD) mice (Zhou & Palmiter, 1995, *Cell*; Szczypka et al., 1999, *PNAS*) cannot self-initiate feeding and starve.** TH inactivation produces mice "severely hypoactive, adipsic, and aphagic," dead by ~3–4 weeks unless rescued by L-DOPA. The cleanest demonstration that, in near-total absence of dopamine, self-initiated **execution** of goal-directed action fails.

2. **Viral restoration of dopamine to the caudate-putamen alone rescues feeding** (Szczypka et al., 2001, *Neuron*; Robinson et al., 2007; Palmiter, 2008, *Ann NY Acad Sci*). Palmiter: dorsal-striatal dopamine is a *permissive signal* gating execution.

3. **What DD mice "preserve" is narrower than the gloss claims.** Sucrose-over-water preference *once a bout is initiated*, morphine CPP (Hnasko et al., 2005, *Nature*), reward-location learning if locomotion is restored by caffeine (Robinson et al., 2005). These concern **learning** and **sensory preference** plus a brainstem **taste reflex** — not subjective pleasure, and not transferable to "human enjoyment is intact."

4. **The "intact liking" measure is a decerebrate-survivable reflex.** Grill & Norgren (1978) taste reactivity — tongue protrusions for sweet, gapes for bitter — is brainstem-mediated and preserved even in decerebrate rats (part II, PMID 630410). Its persistence under dopamine depletion is not a measurement of pleasure; Berridge concedes this by writing "liking" in scare quotes as an objective process *distinct from conscious pleasure* (Berridge & Kringelbach, 2015, *Neuron* 86:646).

5. **Human experimental evidence indicates dopaminergic disruption REDUCES hedonic experience** — the decisive correction to the rodent gloss:
   - **Weber et al. (2023, *J Psychiatr Res*; PMID 36680855):** single 300 mg amisulpride vs. placebo, n=85, double-blind RCT → significantly lower positivity ratings across *all* categories (p=.026) and lower electrodermal responses (p=.017). D2 blockade "can reduce at-the-moment hedonic responsivity in healthy volunteers."
   - **Antipsychotic-induced anhedonia** (PMC4826766): FGAs produce apathy, anhedonia, loss of drive; depression in 20–40%.
   - **Costello et al. (2025, *Brain*; PMC12129732):** PD depression shows markedly lower reward sensitivity, in some forms unresponsive to dopamine treatment.
   - **Wang et al. (PMC4600774):** heavy rhesus dopamine loss impairs morphine CPP — opposite to what a clean dissociation predicts.

6. **The "go-to" human dissociation studies are weak.** Brauer & De Wit (1997), Leyton et al. (2005), Wachtel et al. (2002) used acute tyrosine/phenylalanine depletion (modest DA reduction) and face-valid "high" ratings; small effects, inconsistent replication. The dissociation is cleanest in rodent operant assays; human translation is an open question (Robinson & Berridge, 2025, *Annu Rev Psychol* 76:29).

7. **Akinetic mutism (AM) is the closest human analog — a motor-initiation disorder with preserved consciousness.** Aspen Workgroup (Giacino et al., 2002): "primarily a defect in motor initiation rather than in consciousness." Associated with mesocorticolimbic dopamine pathway / anterior cingulate / dorsomedial thalamus lesions; responsive to L-DOPA, bromocriptine, methylphenidate, amantadine.

8. **Direct evidence AM patients can be conscious despite total unresponsiveness:** Comanducci et al. (2023, *Eur J Neurosci* 59:860–873): PCImax 0.34 > threshold 0.31 (Casarotto et al., 2016), plus fMRI command-following, in a patient behaviorally like VS who recovered on amantadine.

9. **First-person testimony converges on preserved inner experience.** Sacks's *Awakenings* (1973): patients aware through decades of freezing — via spelling board, "I have no exit. I'm trapped in myself. This stupid body is a prison with windows but no doors." Bonifati (2019, *Lancet Neurol*): "aware and observant, at varying degrees." (Contrary datum: Cairns's 1941 patient was amnestic afterward.)

10. **Dopamine encodes multiple parallel signals.** Reward-prediction error (Schultz, Dayan & Montague, 1997); tonic dopamine / response vigor / opportunity cost (Niv et al., 2007); incentive salience (Berridge); direct movement signals — Howe & Dombeck (2016, *Nature* 535:505), ~100 ms bursts "capable of triggering locomotion"; Azcorra et al. (2023, *Nat Neurosci* 26:1762), Aldh1a1⁺ nigrostriatal subtype signaling movement without reward, "especially vulnerable in Parkinson's disease." Salamone & Correa (2012, *Neuron* 76:470) reframe accumbens dopamine as **effort / behavioral activation** — fitting the lived report ("I cannot summon the substrate to engage") better than "I want but don't like."

## Details

### 1. The DD Mouse Literature: Established vs. Interpretation

**Established.** TH-knockout mice (Zhou & Palmiter, 1995, *Cell* 83:1197) become hypoactive, aphagic, adipsic; die ~3–4 weeks unless rescued. L-DOPA restores activity/feeding 6–9 hr/dose (Szczypka et al., 1999, *PNAS* 96:12138). Caudate-putamen-targeted restoration rescues feeding; accumbens-targeted restores exploration but not feeding (Szczypka et al., 2001, *Neuron* 30:819). SNc retrograde Cre restoration rescues for months (Hnasko et al., 2006, *PNAS*).

**Established: action initiation dissociable from learning and sensory preference.** Sucrose preference during managed bouts (Cannon & Palmiter, 2003); morphine CPP (Hnasko et al., 2005); reward learning if caffeinated (Robinson et al., 2005). Palmiter (2008): dorsal-striatal dopamine = permissive gating signal.

**What this does NOT establish.** Not that subjective pleasure is intact. Preserved measures = learning, sensory preference once a bout begins, brainstem taste reflex. None is a readout of felt hedonic experience. "These survive" → "liking/pleasure intact" is the register-jump corrected here.

**Contested framing.** Salamone & Correa (2012): accumbens dopamine governs *effort allocation / behavioral activation*; DA-depleted rats consume free food, pursue low-effort rewards. Niv et al. (2007): tonic dopamine = average reward rate / opportunity cost. Both compatible with permissive gating but reframe deficit as *effortful, self-initiated, costly* action.

### 2. Wanting, Liking, and Whether Human Pleasure Survives Dopamine Loss

**The dissociation, stated correctly.** Berridge, Venier & Robinson (1989): 6-OHDA depletion left orofacial "liking" intact while abolishing feeding. Mu-opioid stimulation of ~1 mm³ hotspots (NAc shell, ventral pallidum) doubles "liking" (Peciña & Berridge, 2005; Smith & Berridge, 2007). Amphetamine/stimulation raise "wanting" not "liking." **This shows wanting and liking are not identical** — a real, useful finding.

**What it does NOT show.** Not that dopamine is irrelevant to pleasure, nor that human enjoyment survives dopamine loss:
- **The "liking" floor is a brainstem reflex** surviving decerebration (Grill & Norgren, 1978, PMID 630410). A component of, not identical to, conscious pleasure — Berridge's own position.
- **Human dopamine manipulation reduces hedonic response** (Weber 2023 RCT; antipsychotic anhedonia, PMC4826766; Costello 2025, *Brain*; Wang, PMC4600774). The strongest *experimental* human data point *against* intact liking.
- **Human dissociation studies are weak** (acute tyrosine depletion, small effects, poor replication).

**Phenomenological point (reasonable synthesis).** Human pleasure includes anticipation, attention, being-drawn-toward as constitutive parts. Strip anticipatory/activational dopamine and you don't isolate "pure liking" — you get anhedonia, the grey deadness of depression and stimulant withdrawal. That is the patient's report, and the report is the stronger datum on a question only a verbal subject can answer.

**Epistemic point for the book.** The field's preference for a clean rodent reflex over convergent human testimony, on a question *about subjective experience*, is a methodological double standard: it elevates an "objective" proxy precisely because it discounts the witness. Not necessarily a conspiracy — the addict-as-unreliable-narrator prior operating inside citation practice. The defensible claim is about *whose report counts as evidence* (true and sharp), not that the rodent work is worthless (overshoot).

### 3. Akinetic Mutism, Advanced Parkinson's, Inner Experience

**Established.** AM = severely reduced movement/speech with preserved wakefulness and brainstem reflexes; frontal-subcortical lesions with dopaminergic pathways implicated (Arnts et al., 2020, *Neurosci Biobehav Rev* 112:270); dopaminergic-responsive.

**Distinctions.** Locked-in (ventral pontine, tracts severed, consciousness uncontroversial, non-dopaminergic); VS / unresponsive wakefulness (no awareness); AM (motor-initiation defect, resembles VS behaviorally, mechanistically distinct).

**Reasonable but contested: consciousness preserved.** Comanducci et al. (2023): PCImax 0.34 > 0.31, fMRI command-following, recovery on amantadine. Preserved ERPs (Naccache 2004; Kotchoubey 2003).

**Uncertain.** AM heterogeneous; Cairns's case amnestic; *Awakenings* literary; no ethical clean test. Defensible: in well-studied dopaminergic-AM cases with preserved cortical processing and full recovery, consciousness was very probably preserved during akinesia.

**Overreach.** Not *every* AM/end-stage PD patient is demonstrably conscious during akinesia; late PD pathology makes some cases unknowable.

### 4. The Motor-Output / Action Distinction: Why Paradoxical Kinesia Does Not Refute Dopamine Necessity

**The empirical facts are not in dispute.** Akinetic Parkinson's patients can move suddenly under existential threat — Bonanni et al. (2010, *Neurol Sci*), 14 patients during the L'Aquila earthquake; Schlesinger et al. (2007), missile attacks. Keefe/Salamone et al. (1989, *Brain Res*): rats with ~98% striatal dopamine depletion swim out of an ice bath, and this behavior is not blocked by haloperidol or SCH-23390, ruling out residual dopamine release as the mechanism. A non-dopaminergic emergency pathway for organized motor output exists.

**What this refutes and what it does not.** Paradoxical kinesia refutes only the crude claim that dopamine is required for every organized motor output. It does not establish dopamine-independent *action* in the ethically or philosophically relevant sense. The distinction is essential.

Vertebrate locomotion is partly implemented by spinal and brainstem pattern generators, reflex loops, proprioceptive feedback, and emergency arousal systems — circuits that can produce organized movement without proving that an agent has initiated sustained conduct from a represented goal. The blunt analogy is post-decapitation locomotion in birds: briefly organized stepping after loss of the forebrain demonstrates that motor organization does not require a head. Paradoxical kinesia is not that crude and should not be reduced to a spinal reflex — it likely involves intact sensory processing, arousal, cueing, and residual higher circuitry. But it makes the same logical point: externally released motor organization is not evidence of ordinary self-initiated agency.

The decisive criterion is the locus of initiation. In paradoxical kinesia, the releasing stimulus is external and supranormal — an earthquake, a missile, ice. Even granting full cortical participation in the motor execution, the *initiation* came from outside the organism, not from the organism's own represented goals. The motor system was unlocked from outside. This is categorically different from the organism unlocking itself from within, which is the thing dopamine deficiency abolishes. **Paradoxical kinesia is cue-released emergency behavior, not a counterexample to dopamine-dependent action.** It shows the motor system can sometimes be triggered from outside. It does not show the agent can initiate from inside.

**Dopamine-independent reward.** DD-mouse morphine CPP (Hnasko et al., 2005); serotonin-mediated cocaine CPP (Hnasko et al., 2007, *J Neurosci* 27:12484). These show reward learning does not require dopamine — consistent with the wanting/liking dissociation — but do not bear on the action-initiation claim, since CPP is a conditioned approach preference, not a demonstration of sustained self-initiated goal-directed conduct.

**Other neuromodulators.** NE, ACh, 5-HT, orexin, and the Schiff central-thalamic mesocircuit all contribute to arousal and motivation. Taylor et al. (2016, *PNAS* 113:12826): VTA-DA optogenetic stimulation restored righting in 6/6 mice under isoflurane; D1 antagonist pretreatment blocked it in 6/6 — sufficient for arousal transitions, though not uniquely required given alternative arousal pathways. These findings complicate any claim that dopamine is the only route to behavioral activation, without touching the claim that it is the necessary route for self-initiated, sustained, goal-directed action under ordinary conditions.

## Recommendations

**High confidence — action initiation:**
> "In rodents, near-total absence of dopamine abolishes self-initiated goal-directed action — animals starve next to available food — and restoring dopamine to the caudate-putamen rescues it (Palmiter, 2008; Szczypka et al., 2001). In humans, akinetic mutism and severe Parkinson's OFF states present a closely analogous picture in which voluntary action is abolished while consciousness and perception appear preserved — shown objectively by TMS-EEG complexity in an AM patient (Comanducci et al., 2023) and consistent with L-DOPA-awakened post-encephalitic testimony (Sacks, 1973)."

**The strong conclusion on action, correctly stated:**
> "Dopamine is necessary for all action in the ethically relevant sense: the self-initiated, sustained conversion of represented need, preference, goal, or reason into conduct. Near-total dopamine absence does not abolish every externally-released or reflexively-triggered behavior. It abolishes the organism's ordinary capacity to initiate and sustain goal-directed life."

The apparent exceptions (paradoxical kinesia, residual swimming, serotonin-mediated CPP) do not contradict this conclusion. They show that the motor system can sometimes be unlocked from outside under supranormal conditions, and that reward learning can proceed without dopamine. Neither demonstrates that the organism can unlock itself from within — which is the thing the claim is about, the thing the law assumes when it assigns blame, and the thing the anhedonic trap destroys.

**High confidence — corrects the popular meme on liking:**
> "The claim that dopamine loss 'preserves liking' does not hold for human pleasure. The rodent 'liking' measure is a brainstem taste reflex surviving decerebration, not a readout of felt pleasure (Grill & Norgren, 1978; Berridge concedes the distinction). Human dopamine blockade *reduces* hedonic responsivity (Weber et al., 2023), antipsychotics induce anhedonia, and Parkinson's reward insensitivity is real and sometimes dopamine-unresponsive (Costello et al., 2025). In humans, anhedonia is a genuine loss of pleasure, not an illusion masking intact enjoyment."

**Moderate confidence:**
> "Wanting and liking are partially separable — a real opioid-mediated hedonic component dissociable from dopaminergic wanting under specific manipulations — but they normally operate together, and dopamine is the substrate of wanting, effort, behavioral activation, and the conversion of value into initiated action."

**Avoid:**
> "Dopamine isn't pleasure / liking is intact" as applied to humans — unsupported, and the framing used to dismiss anhedonia as theatrical. Also avoid asserting that *every* AM or end-stage PD patient is demonstrably conscious during akinesia — late PD pathology makes some cases unknowable.

## Caveats

- **Species translation cuts against the gloss.** The "liking preserved" evidence is rodent; human evidence (Weber, Costello, Wang) points the other way. Extrapolating rodent intact-liking to humans is the fragile step.
- **Terminological landmine.** Motivation / wanting / liking / valence / pleasure / agency / voluntary action are not interchangeable; much apparent disagreement is about term-to-signal mapping, not facts.
- **"Liking" in scare quotes ≠ pleasure.** Berridge is explicit. Dropping the quotes to assert "pleasure intact" is a register error.
- **Consciousness-preserved is inferential.** PCI is a correlate; Cairns's amnestic case leaves the universal claim open.
- **Threshold/compensation.** Behavior largely unaffected until >80% depletion (Palmiter 2008); the claim concerns *near-total* absence.
- **Bibliographic note.** The multimodal AM study is Comanducci, Casarotto, Rosanova, … Massimini (2023), *Eur J Neurosci* 59(5):860–873. Arnts leads the 2020 *Neurosci Biobehav Rev* review and a 2024 *Sci Rep* DBS-zolpidem case.
