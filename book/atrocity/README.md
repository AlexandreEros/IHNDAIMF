# Part III — The Atrocity

This part turns from the biology of agency to its political interpretation. It asks what happens when failures of motivation, reward, and action are treated not as properties of vulnerable biological systems but as vice, defiance, dangerousness, or moral contamination.

The rhetoric becomes deliberately sharper here, but the project's four epistemic registers still apply: documented fact, synthesis, speculative phenomenology, and literary image should remain distinguishable.
