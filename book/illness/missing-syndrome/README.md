# The Missing Syndrome

This node collects the historical and clinical evidence around long-term stimulant recovery, with special attention to what has and has not actually been measured.

It moves from Japan's postwar Philopon crisis and non-English follow-up literature into modern recovery evidence, D2/D3 receptor findings, and the possibility that clinically important post-stimulant dysfunction is fragmented across labels rather than recognized as a coherent syndrome.

Absence of evidence is kept distinct from evidence of irreversibility.
